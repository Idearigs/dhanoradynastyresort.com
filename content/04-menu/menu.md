# Menu — Dhanora Dynasty Resort

**SOURCE OF TRUTH.** Transcribed from the owner's printed menu (photos received 13 July 2026).

This **supersedes** the old site entirely. The old `/menu` page listed generic Western dishes
(Grilled Salmon, Tiramisu, Crème Brûlée, Beef Tenderloin) that the resort does not serve — it was
template filler. The real menu is a Sri Lankan / international blend, below.

> **Prices are recorded here but are NOT published on the website** (owner's instruction).
> They exist for the admin panel / the `MenuItem.price` field in `docs/PROJECT-PLAN.md`.
> The public site renders names + descriptions only — see `src/lib/menu.ts`.

> **Spelling:** the printed menu has many typos ("Whaite Basmathi", "Sncks", "Uegetable",
> "Spa Ghetti Cabonara", "Balck Tea", "Deveilled"). The website uses corrected spelling.
> ⚠️ **Confirm with the owner** that corrected spelling is acceptable.

All prices in LKR (Rs.). "—" = not priced on the printed menu.
