## Licence obligations

- **CC BY 4.0 / CC BY-SA 3.0 / 4.0** — must credit the author and link the licence. CC-BY-SA
  also requires that any modified version be shared under the same licence.
- **FAL (Free Art License)** — must credit the author and keep the work free.
- **CC0** — public domain, no attribution required (Mihintale), but crediting is courteous.

The cleanest way to satisfy this: add a short "Image credits" page listing the rows above with
links to each Commons page and licence, and link it from the footer.
