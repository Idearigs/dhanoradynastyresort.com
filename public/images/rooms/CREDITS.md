# Room image credits

Four photos per room (`<room-no>-1..4.webp`), from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), free for
commercial use. Each is 1000x700 WebP (~50 KB; 1.2 MB for all 24).

`-1` is the card thumbnail on /rooms; all four appear in the View Details carousel.

## THESE ARE PLACEHOLDERS

They are generic hotel-room stock and do **not** show the actual resort. Room 106 is described
as having "rare views of ancient pagodas and a tranquil lake" - no stock photo can show that.
Replace these with real photos of the six rooms as soon as the owner supplies them.

To swap: drop a 1000x700 WebP over the matching filename. No code change needed - the page
derives the paths from the room number. To change how many photos a room has, edit `photos()`
in `src/routes/rooms.tsx`.
