## Replacing with the resort's own photos

Drop a 400x300 WebP over the matching filename. To ADD a photo for one of the gaps above, save
it as the slugified dish name (e.g. `milk-rice.webp`) and add that slug to `DISH_IMAGES` in
`src/lib/menu.ts`.
